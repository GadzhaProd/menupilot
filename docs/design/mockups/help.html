What MenuPilot does

MenuPilot shows what your recipes cost and lets you try “what if” changes before updating real prices or recipes.

You can start with the demo café. Its dishes and prices are fictional.

Contribution is not profit

Contribution (before other costs) is what remains after recipe-based ingredient cost is taken away from the menu price excluding VAT.

€12.00 price excl. VAT
minus €4.85 recipe-based ingredient cost excl. VAT
= €7.15 contribution

Labour, rent and all other costs still need to be paid from this amount.

Add a new supplier price

  1. Open Ingredients and find the ingredient.
  2. Choose New price. Enter the pack size and supplier price excluding VAT, as on the invoice.
  3. Check the unit and choose Add price. The old price stays in history.
  4. Open Dashboard to see which dishes changed.

Try a scenario safely

Open Simulator for a dish. Baseline is today's real data. A, B and C are separate what-if copies.

Your monthly sales estimate is your own number. MenuPilot does not predict demand.

Keep a backup

Your data lives in this browser. Use Data → Export backup to keep a copy. A failed import leaves the existing data unchanged.

Вклад — это не прибыль

После вычета ингредиентов остаётся вклад до прочих расходов. Из него ещё нужно оплатить работу, аренду и другие расходы.
